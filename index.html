02 / WHAT I’VE BUILT

DISTRIBUTED SYSTEMS · RELIABILITY

AtlasDistributed job scheduler

Sep 2026 – Present

Atlas is a distributed job scheduler I’m building in Go with PostgreSQL and Redis. I designed it to keep work moving when workers fail and to coordinate job claims across multiple scheduler instances.

I tested lease-based recovery by running 1,000 tasks across 10 workers and forcibly terminating one worker. Heartbeat-based failure detection reassigned its in-flight tasks within 10 seconds, validating that unfinished work could recover without manual intervention.

For delivery and failure handling, Atlas supports at-least-once task delivery, configurable retries with exponential backoff for transient errors, and dead-letter queues for tasks that cannot complete. Leader election and database locking coordinate scheduler instances and prevent duplicate scheduling during concurrent job claims.

FULL-STACK ENGINEERING · APPLIED AI

FitCoreAI-powered fitness platform

Jan 2026 – Apr 2026

FitCore is a full-stack fitness and nutrition platform built around coaching conversations and easier food logging. People can describe food in text, use voice transcripts, or add food photos; the food-logging flow reached 91% recognition accuracy while reducing manual entry.

For nutrition extraction from typed and transcribed descriptions, I fine-tuned a lightweight model with LoRA/SFT. Compared with a large-model baseline, it reduced inference cost by 75% and latency from 600 ms to under 150 ms, while keeping extraction accuracy within one percentage point of the baseline.

The coaching experience uses hybrid retrieval: BM25 and vector search are combined with RRF fusion, then a cross-encoder reranks the results. When a question is ambiguous, the system uses conversation context to rewrite it, adjusts the retrieval top-k to the question complexity and corpus size, and selects evidence for source-cited answers.

To personalize conversations across sessions, I added token-budgeted context and rolling summaries, and stored fitness goals and dietary preferences in user profiles. This lets coaching use relevant history without resending full chat transcripts.
